Quick Puz – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Quick Puz mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Quick Puz is a collection of puzzle games: Sliding Tiles, Lights Out, Memory Match, Flood It, Picross and more.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Quick Puz does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as cleared levels, stars earned, and best results) locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

3. Permissions

Quick Puz does not request any sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

4. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not share any data with third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Quick Puz is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

Since your progress is stored only on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
